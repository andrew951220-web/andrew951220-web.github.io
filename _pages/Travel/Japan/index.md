# 🇯🇵 Japan Travel

Welcome to my Japan travel logs. This page lists my explorations, food guides, and travel itineraries across different regions of Japan.

---

## 📍 My Travel Map

Here are the cities I have explored and plan to visit:

*   **Tokyo (도쿄):** The perfect mix of futuristic technology and historical landmarks.
*   **Kyoto (교토):** Beautiful temples, traditional tea houses, and stunning autumn foliage.
*   **Osaka (오사카):** The kitchen of Japan – famous for takoyaki, okonomiyaki, and vibrant nightlife.
*   **Hokkaido (훗카이도):** Winter wonderland, famous for hot springs (onsen) and fresh seafood.

---

## 🍜 Top Eats in Japan
My personal list of must-try dishes and recommended restaurants:

1.  **Ichiran Ramen (Tonkotsu):** A classic favorite for rich pork-bone broth.
2.  **Gyukatsu Motomura:** Crispy deep-fried beef cutlet that you grill on a stone plate.
3.  **Kushikatsu (Osaka):** Deep-fried skewers of meat, seafood, and vegetables.
4.  **Kyoto Matcha Treats:** Ice cream and traditional sweets in Uji.

---

## 💡 Quick Travel Tips for Japan
*   **Suica/Pasmo:** Get a digital IC card on your phone for easy train rides and convenience store payments.
*   **Google Maps:** Extremely accurate for train schedules, platform numbers, and walking routes.
*   **Cash is King:** Although cards are widely accepted now, keep some cash for small street vendors and local ramen ticket machines.
